02 — VERIFICATION LIFECYCLE

How it works

A seamless, tamper-evident digital workflow for legal metrology compliance.

STAGE 01

Register

Enter instrument specifications — category, make, model, capacity, verification scale interval, and serial number. Keep your entire equipment inventory in a structured ledger.

STAGE 02

Request

Submit a verification filing with a preferred inspection date. The controller's office reviews the request and assigns an authorized legal metrology officer.

STAGE 03

Inspect

The inspector visits on-site and inputs test readings into the digital inspection console. Tolerances are validated dynamically against statutory maximum permissible error rules.

STAGE 04

Certify

Upon passing inspection, an official Certificate of Verification is generated in real time with a cryptographically unique token, sequential number, and 35mm scannable QR stamp.
